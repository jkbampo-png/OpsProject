# Operations Analytics Case Study
### Power BI | Process Improvement | Customer Journey Analytics

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how operational analytics can be used to improve customer retention by identifying bottlenecks throughout the customer journey.

The dashboard was designed to replace manual reporting with KPI-driven decision making by tracking the movement of qualified leads through activation and long-term retention.

---

# Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard provides leadership with a high-level view of operational performance.

It monitors:

- Total Leads
- Qualified Leads
- Activated Customers
- Retained Customers
- Weekly pipeline activity
- Funnel conversion rates
- Individual customer records requiring intervention

The objective is to allow decision makers to quickly identify where performance is improving and where operational attention is required.

![Executive Dashboard](01-Executive-Dashboard.png)

---

# Conversion Analysis

This page analyses how efficiently qualified leads progress through the customer lifecycle.

Key metrics include:

- Lead-to-Activation Rate
- Lead-to-Retention Rate
- Average Days to Activation
- Monthly Cohort Performance
- Conversion Trends

Rather than simply reporting totals, this page highlights where conversion efficiency changes over time and helps identify opportunities for process improvement.

![Conversion Analysis](02-Conversion-Analysis.png)

---

# Lead Segmentation

This page explores how different demographic groups move through the qualification process.

The analysis compares:

- Overall Lead Demographics
- Qualification Rates
- Segment Distribution

Segmenting qualified leads provides insight into which groups progress most successfully through the pipeline and where engagement strategies may need to be tailored.

![Lead Segmentation](03-Lead-Segmentation.png)

---

# Segment Performance

This page measures operational performance across different customer segments after qualification.

It compares:

- Qualified Lead Demographics
- Activated Customer Segments
- Activation Rates by Segment

Comparing activation performance across segments allows operational teams to identify where follow-up processes are most effective and where additional engagement may be required.

![Segment Performance](04-Segment-Performance.png)
